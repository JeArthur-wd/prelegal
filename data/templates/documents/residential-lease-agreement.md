The Premises shall be used only as a private residence and occupied only by the Tenant and the following additional occupants: {{additional_occupants}}. The Tenant shall not sublet or assign the Lease without the Landlord's prior written consent.

The Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and in good condition and shall promptly notify the Landlord of any needed repairs. The Landlord shall maintain the structure, plumbing, heating, and electrical systems in good working order, except for damage caused by the Tenant or the Tenant's guests.

## 8. Pets

Pet policy: {{pet_policy}}.

## 9. Entry by Landlord

The Landlord may enter the Premises at reasonable times, with reasonable advance notice as required by law, to inspect, make repairs, or show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers. In an emergency, the Landlord may enter without notice.

## 10. Alterations

The Tenant shall not make alterations, paint, or install fixtures without the Landlord's prior written consent.

## 11. Default

If the Tenant fails to pay rent when due or otherwise breaches this Lease, the Landlord may pursue any remedy permitted by law, including termination of the Lease, after giving any notice required by law.

## 12. Move-Out

At the end of the tenancy, the Tenant shall return all keys and leave the Premises in the same condition as at the start of the Lease, except for normal wear and tear.
