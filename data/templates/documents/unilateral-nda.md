# Non-Disclosure Agreement (One-Way)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of {{effective_date}} (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**{{discloser_name}}**, with its principal address at {{discloser_address}} (the "Discloser"); and

**{{recipient_name}}**, with its principal address at {{recipient_address}} (the "Recipient").

## 1. Purpose

The Discloser intends to share certain confidential information with the Recipient solely for the purpose of {{purpose}} (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public business, technical, financial, and other information disclosed by the Discloser to the Recipient, in any form, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can demonstrate:

1. is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient;
2. was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure;
3. is lawfully obtained from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or
4. is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Confidential Information.

## 3. Recipient's Obligations

The Recipient shall:

1. use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
2. not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party without the Discloser's prior written consent, except to its personnel and advisors who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations;
3. protect the Confidential Information with at least reasonable care; and
4. promptly notify the Discloser of any unauthorized use or disclosure.

## 4. Compelled Disclosure

If the Recipient is legally compelled to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall, where legally permitted, give the Discloser prompt written notice and cooperate with any effort by the Discloser to obtain a protective order.

## 5. Return of Materials

On the Discloser's written request, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all materials containing Confidential Information.

## 6. Duration

The Recipient's obligations under this Agreement continue for {{confidentiality_period_years}} year(s) from the Effective Date.

## 7. No License

No license or other right to the Confidential Information is granted to the Recipient except as expressly stated in this Agreement.

## 8. Remedies

The Recipient acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the Discloser irreparable harm, and that the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy available at law.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of {{governing_law}}.

## 10. Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and may be modified only in a writing signed by both parties.
